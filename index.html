02The Game

A top-down pixel-art souls-like comedy, made in Godot 4.

Souls-like combat

Dodge, parry, stamina. Every boss has a tell.

Comedy story

Tyrone roasts every boss on the way to Johnny Blipp.

PC + Android

Touch controls, same look on phone. Latest build: 36.

Tyrone does not care. Tyrone wants to go home.

04Bosses & Enemies

Godskin Hunchback

A walking boulder with a skull. Barely speaks. Hits like a truck, thinks like a rock.

Tekitou Teru

Some random guy you are forced to kill. Mandatory, forgettable, and he knows it. Art by Kurt.

Newliv

Desert king. Never shuts up, flirts with everything that breathes and it is deeply creepy. Has a phase 2.

Shinobi

Secret boss with a dark aura. Plenty of power, no honour: at low health he throws pocket sand.

Sarge Nipis

Gold armour, big muscles, tiny brain. Lures himself onto his own burning floor.

???

Johnny Blipp

The final boss. Loses in his own castle.

Tyrone has an insult ready for every one of them.

Plus husks, brutes and slimes on the road. Build 36: Pezlo reveals a view cone, everything outside it dims.
