01The Game

A top-down pixel-art souls-like, made in Godot 4.
The hero would rather be anywhere else.

Souls-like combat

Dodge, parry, stamina. Every boss shows a tell first.

A hero who doesn't care

Tyrone insults every boss between him and his front door.

PC + Android

Keyboard, gamepad or touch. Same game, same look on a phone.

Tyrone does not care. Tyrone wants to go home.
